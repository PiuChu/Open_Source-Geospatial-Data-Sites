# Open_Source-Geospatial-Data-Sites

Stop spending 40% of your research cycle hunting down fragmented datasets.

Whether you are parameterising a mechanistic ecohydrological model, running multi-decadal land cover change detection, validating satellite retrievals against eddy-covariance flux towers, or mapping ancient landscapes, data discovery shouldn't be the bottleneck.

Here is a compiled Master **Directory of Free & Open-Source Geospatial Data Warehouses** spanning the entire Earth observation, environmental, socioeconomic, and archaeological spectrum, complete with direct links to the primary portals and STAC catalogues.

Bookmark this for your next workflow:
